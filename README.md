## Problem Statement 
Access to clean drinking water and hygienic restroom facilities in public spaces (railway stations, bus stands, markets, parks, and government offices) remains inconsistent and poorly monitored across many Indian cities and towns. Citizens especially women, elderly people, and persons with disabilities often face unhygienic, non-functional, or entirely absent facilities, with no structured system to report issues or track maintenance. Authorities lack real-time, localized data on the condition and availability of these amenities, which delays repairs and accountability. There is a need for a technology-driven system that enables citizens to report, rate, and track the status of public restrooms and drinking water points, while giving municipal bodies actionable, data-backed insights to prioritize maintenance and new installations.

## Objectives 
- To assess the current availability, accessibility, and hygiene condition of public restrooms and drinking water facilities in the target area.
- To design a platform (app/web-based) that allows citizens to report issues, rate facilities, and view nearby available amenities in real time.
- To create a centralized dashboard for municipal/civic authorities to monitor complaints, identify high-priority locations, and track resolution status.
- To apply data analysis (and ML, if in scope) to identify patterns — e.g., frequently reported locations, peak complaint periods, or facility clusters needing urgent attention.
- To improve public health outcomes and civic accountability by reducing response time between issue reporting and resolution.
- To reduce the response time between issue reporting and resolution through automated alerts and complaint prioritization.
- To raise citizen awareness and encourage community participation in maintaining public sanitation infrastructure.
